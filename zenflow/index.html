What is ZenFlow?

ZenFlow presents itself as a productivity and wellness app: a calm place to focus and build a healthier daily rhythm, rather than another noisy feed.

It is a Vercel-hosted web app. Use it in the browser when you want a slower, more focused session — for work, study or a wellness break — and pair it with Fit Coach for workouts and Weight Loss Calendar for tracking if you want a full health routine.

Key features

  • Productivity and wellness in one app
  • Calm, focused experience designed for daily rhythm
  • Web-based — open it in the browser
  • Pairs well with Fit Coach and Weight Loss Calendar for a full routine

Who is it for?

Anyone who wants calmer focus and a healthier daily rhythm, at work or at home.

How to use ZenFlow

  1. Open ZenFlow from the button below.
  2. Start a calm session in the app.
  3. Use it for focused work, study or a wellness break.
  4. Return daily to build the rhythm.
