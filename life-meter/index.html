What is Life Meter?

Life Meter is a web app that calculates wasted life time and remaining life expectancy from daily habits. Its own line says it best: this is not motivation, this is reality.

Enter your age, daily phone hours and daily study or work hours, and it shows what those habits add up to over a lifetime — a shock that, for many users, is the point.

Key features

  • Life-wasted and life-remaining calculation
  • Inputs for age, phone hours and study/work hours
  • Instant, numbers-first results
  • Built to make screen time feel concrete, not abstract
  • Web-based, no install needed

Who is it for?

Anyone who suspects phone time is eating their years and wants the number, not a quote.

How to use Life Meter

  1. Open Life Meter from the button below.
  2. Enter your age.
  3. Enter your daily phone hours and daily study or work hours.
  4. Read your wasted-time and remaining-time numbers — then decide what to change.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to enter?

Is Life Meter medical advice?

No. It is a habit calculator and reality check, not a medical or life-expectancy diagnosis.
