What is DailyScope?

DailyScope is a daily news and reading site. Instead of doom-scrolling many feeds, it brings the day's articles together in one place, organised by topic.

Its sections cover Politics, Business and Economy, Sports, Entertainment, World, Health and Education, Cities and States, and Auto and Tech, with a library of hundreds of articles and a daily quiz to test what you read.

Key features

  • Daily articles across 8 news sections
  • Politics, business, sports, entertainment, world and more
  • Cities and States coverage for Indian readers
  • A daily quiz alongside the news
  • A growing library of hundreds of articles

Who is it for?

Readers who want one daily read across news topics, and students or quiz lovers who want a daily test.

How to use DailyScope

  1. Open DailyScope from the button below.
  2. Pick a section — Politics, Sports, Entertainment, or any other.
  3. Read the day's articles.
  4. Try the daily quiz to check what you retained.
