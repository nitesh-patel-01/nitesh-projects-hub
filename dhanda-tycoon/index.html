What is Dhanda Tycoon?

Dhanda Tycoon is a free Indian business simulator game. You start with Rs 10,000, survive daily expenses, read market moods, build streaks and try to build a Rs 1 lakh empire — or go bankrupt trying.

You choose a business to run: a Chai Stall (easy start), a Freelance Agency (medium), a Clothing Brand (hard) or a YouTube Channel (extreme). Each has different overheads, risks and ceilings, and the game needs no download.

Key features

  • Start with Rs 10,000, win at Rs 1,00,000
  • Daily expenses, market moods and streak bonuses
  • 4 businesses: chai stall, freelance agency, clothing brand, YouTube channel
  • Difficulty from Easy to Extreme
  • No download needed — play in the browser
  • Leaderboard of top business tycoons

Who is it for?

Players who like business, strategy and Indian street-smart decision games.

How to use Dhanda Tycoon

  1. Open Dhanda Tycoon from the button below.
  2. Choose your business and difficulty.
  3. Make a decision each day: survive expenses, grow reputation and customers.
  4. Reach Rs 1,00,000 to become a Tycoon — or go bankrupt and try again.

Frequently asked questions

Is Dhanda Tycoon free?

Yes. It is a free browser game with no download needed.

How do you win Dhanda Tycoon?

Build your business from Rs 10,000 to Rs 1,00,000 without going bankrupt.

Which business should a beginner pick?

The Chai Stall — it is marked as the easy start, with low overhead and a loyal base.
