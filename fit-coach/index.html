What is Fit Coach?

Fit Coach is a free AI personal trainer built for India. It gives gym and home workouts, Indian veg and non-veg diet plans, an Indian-English voice coach, and progress, BMI and streak tracking — all in one private app.

Everything is stored locally on your device using browser storage. There are no accounts, no subscriptions and no ads on the site, and after the first load it works offline in the browser.

Key features

  • Gym and home workout plans, by goal and experience level
  • Indian veg and non-veg diet plans — paneer, dal, soya chunks, chicken, eggs and more
  • Indian-English voice coach
  • Progress, BMI, weight, water and streak tracking
  • 100% private: data stays on your device, no account needed

Who is it for?

Beginners to advanced users in India who want a workout plus an Indian diet plan, at home or in the gym.

How to use Fit Coach

  1. Open Fit Coach from the button below.
  2. Answer the setup questions: weight, height, goal, workout type and diet preference.
  3. Get your weekly workout schedule and daily diet plan.
  4. Log weight, water and workouts to build your streak.

Frequently asked questions

Is Fit Coach free?

Yes. Fit Coach is free, with no ads, subscriptions or account required, according to the site.

Does Fit Coach work at home without equipment?

Yes. Choose Home workout mode and you get bodyweight-only exercises such as push-ups, squats, lunges and planks.

Is my Fit Coach data private?

Yes. Everything is stored locally on your device in browser storage, with no servers or accounts.
