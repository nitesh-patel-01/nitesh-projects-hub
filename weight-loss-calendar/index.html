What is Weight Loss Calendar?

Weight Loss Calendar is a personal fitness companion for tracking, monitoring and achieving weight management goals. It combines a calendar view with progress charts and detailed statistics to keep you motivated.

All data stays on your device in browser storage — no cloud storage, no tracking. You can export your history as CSV to analyse, back up or share with a healthcare provider. The site itself states it is a tracking tool only, not a medical device or a substitute for professional advice.

Key features

  • Daily weight logging on a calendar, with notes on mood, meals or workouts
  • Colour-coded trends: green for loss, red for gain
  • Statistics: entries, current weight, lowest weight and total progress
  • 30-day progress charts
  • CSV export for backup or sharing
  • 100% private: data stays on your device
  • Works on desktop, tablet and mobile

Who is it for?

Anyone starting or maintaining a weight journey who wants simple, private daily tracking.

How to use Weight Loss Calendar

  1. Open Weight Loss Calendar from the button below.
  2. Click any day in the calendar and enter your weight, with an optional note.
  3. Watch entries appear with colour-coded day-to-day change.
  4. Check the Stats tab and 30-day chart, and export CSV as a backup.

Frequently asked questions

Is my weight data private?

Yes. All data is stored locally on your device and is never sent to any server, according to the site.

Can I use it on multiple devices?

Data is stored locally per device, but you can export CSV and carry it elsewhere.

Is Weight Loss Calendar medical advice?

No. It is a tracking tool only, not a medical device or a substitute for professional healthcare advice.
