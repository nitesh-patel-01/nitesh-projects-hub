What is Pro Resume Builder 2026?

Pro Resume Builder is a free, browser-based resume and cover letter maker for job seekers from fresh graduates to experienced professionals.

It offers 6 professional ATS-optimised templates — Classic, Modern Blue, Elegant Serif, Ultra Minimal, Bold Dark and Pure ATS — with live preview as you type, a resume quality score, a matching cover letter builder and instant PDF download. Your progress auto-saves in your browser, and no signup is needed.

Key features

  • 6 professional, ATS-optimised templates
  • Live real-time preview as you type
  • Resume quality score with improvement tips
  • Cover letter builder with matching design
  • Instant PDF download, no account needed
  • Auto-save to browser local storage
  • 100% free, no paywall

Who is it for?

Freshers, job switchers and experienced professionals who need a clean, ATS-friendly CV fast.

How to use Pro Resume Builder 2026

  1. Open Pro Resume Builder from the button below.
  2. Fill in your details: name, job title, experience, education and skills.
  3. Pick one of the 6 templates and adjust the accent colour.
  4. Check the live preview and quality score, then download your PDF.
