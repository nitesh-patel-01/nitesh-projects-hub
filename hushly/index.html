What is Hushly?

Hushly is an anonymous-questions app in the NGL style: make your link, share it on Instagram or WhatsApp, and post your answers to your Story.

Friends create an account to ask, and can choose to stay anonymous to you. It also offers Instagram Story question ideas and common questions to get you started.

Key features

  • Create your own Hushly link in minutes
  • Share on Instagram or WhatsApp
  • Friends can ask anonymously
  • Post answers to your Story
  • Story question ideas and common questions included

Who is it for?

Instagram and WhatsApp users who want fun, honest questions from friends — and Story content from the answers.

How to use Hushly

  1. Open Hushly from the button below.
  2. Create your link.
  3. Share it on Instagram or WhatsApp.
  4. Read the questions you get and post your answers to your Story.
