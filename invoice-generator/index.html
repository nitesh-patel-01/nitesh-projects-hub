What is Invoice Generator?

Invoice Generator, also called InvoiceMaker Pro on the site, is a free browser-based invoice maker built for Indian freelancers, small businesses, consultants and agencies.

It supports GSTIN, HSN/SAC codes and per-item tax and discount, plus logo upload, multi-currency billing and one-click PDF download. All data stays in your browser — nothing is sent to a server.

Key features

  • GST-ready invoices: GSTIN, HSN/SAC, per-item tax
  • Logo upload for branded invoices
  • Per-item discount and tax, unlimited line items
  • One-click PDF download and copy-as-text
  • Multi-currency: INR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED and more
  • Paid, Unpaid and Overdue status stamps
  • 100% private: data never leaves your browser

Who is it for?

Freelancers, boutique owners, consultants, contractors and small agencies in India who bill clients regularly.

How to use Invoice Generator

  1. Open Invoice Generator from the button below.
  2. Fill in your business details and your client's details, including GSTIN where needed.
  3. Add items with quantity, rate, tax and discount.
  4. Preview the invoice, then download it as PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Is this invoice generator really free?

Yes. It is free, with no signup, hidden charges or watermarks on invoices, according to the site.

Can I create GST invoices here?

Yes. Add GSTIN, client GSTIN, HSN/SAC codes and per-item tax percentages for GST-compliant invoices.

Is my invoice data safe?

Yes. All invoice data is processed entirely in your browser and is not stored or sent to any server.
