What is Jabalpur Navratri Pandals?

Jabalpur Navratri Pandals is a festival guide to Navratri in Jabalpur, the city known as Sanskardhani. It lists 117 pandals across 53 areas, so you can find your next Durga and Mahakali darshan by neighbourhood.

Each pandal page gives its area, aagman and maha aarti details where published, and a route-planning view. Where a time is listed as awaiting update, the site says to check the samiti's Instagram — schedules are a directory snapshot, not live crowd monitoring, so confirm before travelling.

Key features

  • 117 pandals to explore, organised into 53 areas
  • Durga and Mahakali darshan listings
  • Aagman and maha aarti schedules where published
  • Route planning: save favourites and plan an evening of darshan
  • Area-by-area browsing: Garha, Sadar, Ranjhi and more
  • Installable web app: saved pandals stay on your device

Who is it for?

Devotees and visitors in Jabalpur during Navratri who want to plan darshan by area, not wander blindly.

How to use Jabalpur Navratri Pandals

  1. Open Jabalpur Navratri Pandals from the button below.
  2. Pick your area — or browse all 117 pandals.
  3. Open a pandal for its aagman and aarti details.
  4. Save favourites, plan a route, and confirm timings with the samiti before you head out.

Frequently asked questions

How many pandals does the guide list?

117 pandals across 53 areas of Jabalpur.

Are aarti timings live?

No. The site is a directory snapshot — crowd and queue times are not monitored live, and times marked awaiting update need checking with the samiti.

Can I plan a route?

Yes. Save favourites and build an evening route from the guide.
