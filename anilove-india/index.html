What is AniLove India?

AniLove India is a legal streaming directory for India. It does not host pirated video: it plays official YouTube embeds from reviewed channels such as Muse Asia, Muse India, Muse Hindi Dub, TMS, KBS World, Goldmines, Shemaroo, Gundam and Pokemon Asia Hindi.

Its catalogue covers anime, anime movies, Hindi movies and K-dramas, including complete Hindi-dubbed seasons — for example JoJo's Bizarre Adventure in Hindi and the complete Assassination Classroom Season 1 in Hindi.

Key features

  • Only official, reviewed YouTube embeds
  • Anime, anime movies, Hindi movies and K-dramas
  • Complete Hindi-dubbed seasons where available
  • Episode guides and discover pages by series and language
  • Free to watch, with no pirated uploads

Who is it for?

Anime fans in India who want legal Hindi dubs and official uploads, not pirated sites.

How to use AniLove India

  1. Open AniLove India from the button below.
  2. Search or browse the catalogue by title, language or discover page.
  3. Pick a season and episode.
  4. Watch the official YouTube embed on the page.
