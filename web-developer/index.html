What is Nitesh Patel — Web Developer?

This is Nitesh Patel's freelance web-developer site. Its headline promise is a website that brings you customers, and its primary offer is a free homepage draft for your business.

It is aimed at local businesses — cafes, gyms, salons, coaching centres, clinics, shops and property businesses — with a short lead form (name and contact only) right after the hero, a three-step Send, See draft, Decide process, and examples of real work including Toolora and the Jabalpur Navratri directory. No prices are published on this site.

Local business owners who want a website that brings enquiries, and want to see a free draft before deciding.

How to use Nitesh Patel — Web Developer

  1. Open the Web Developer site from the button below.
